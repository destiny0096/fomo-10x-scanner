### Acquire an existing Solana-focused software project

FOMO 10X Scanner is a Python-based Solana token-discovery and monitoring project built around configurable market filters, token-evaluation modules and automated Telegram alerts.

The codebase brings together DexScreener market data, market-activity scoring, security checks, liquidity-pool assessment, sell-route evaluation and a separate token-tracking component.

The acquisition gives the new owner an existing technology foundation to operate, customize and develop in their own direction.

### What the project includes

**Automated token discovery**
- Solana token candidate discovery using DexScreener public market data.
- Configurable market-cap, liquidity and volume filters.
- Transaction activity, buy-pressure and momentum evaluation.
- Configurable scoring thresholds and polling interval.

**Token evaluation modules**
- Mint and freeze authority checks.
- Token-program and token-configuration checks.
- Permanent-delegate and transfer-fee configuration checks.
- Liquidity-pool status evaluation.
- Jupiter sell-route availability checks.

**Telegram alert system**
- Automated Telegram watch alerts.
- Market-cap, liquidity and volume information.
- Evaluation score and token-check information.

**Separate tracking component**
- SQLite-backed token tracking.
- Cohort recording and market-cap monitoring.
- Reporting utilities.
- Backup and restore utilities.
