# FOMO 10X Scanner — Historical Performance Evidence Register

## Purpose

This register is the source-of-truth worksheet for compiling the scanner's historical Results 1, Results 2, and Results 3. Only figures supported by the original logs, screenshots, exports, or contemporaneous records should be published.

## Evidence currently available in project notes

The project notes contain historical summaries for earlier scanner tests, including a first 100-alert test and a subsequent 100-alert test. Those summaries are not a substitute for the original token-by-token records. The exact Results 1/2/3 mapping, cohort boundaries, entry market caps, observation windows, and calculation method must be reconciled against the original records before publication.

## Required fields for each recorded token

- Token name and symbol as recorded.
- Contract/mint address.
- Alert date and timestamp, including timezone.
- Starting market cap and its source.
- Follow-up/current or peak market cap.
- Multiple calculation and observation window.
- Whether the token was included in the defined cohort.
- Source artifact (CSV, log, screenshot, or other original record).

## Calculation convention

multiple = observed_market_cap / starting_market_cap

Do not mix market capitalization with FDV. If the original source used FDV or an unclear basis, flag that row and do not silently combine it with market-cap results.

## Required summary metrics

Once the original records are recovered, calculate:
- Number of records in the cohort.
- Number and percentage below 1x.
- Number and percentage at or above 1x, 2x, 3x, 5x and 10x.
- Highest observed multiple and its source row.
- Median multiple, if all rows have comparable observation windows.
- Missing or unverifiable rows.
- Observation period and data-capture method.

## Evidence handling rules

- Preserve the original records unchanged.
- Keep raw evidence separate from calculated summaries.
- Record the calculation method and any exclusions.
- Do not infer missing values.
- Do not claim independent verification unless an independent party has performed it.
- Do not publish a performance total until the cohort count and all included rows have been reconciled.
