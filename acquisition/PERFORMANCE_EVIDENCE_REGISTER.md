Across the three historical tests, the standout recorded peaks were **14x, 67x and 15x**. Results 2 also recorded four 10x+ examples: 67x, 17x, 16x and 11x. Results 3 recorded milestones at 10x, 13x, 14x and 15x.

These highlights are intended to give prospective buyers a clear view of the positive milestones recorded by the scanner and the potential starting point for their own continued development and testing.

## Calculation basis

The multiple is calculated as:

`observed market cap ÷ starting market cap`

The historical summaries use market-cap multiples. The original token-level source records can be used during the acquisition handover to review individual examples and calculations.
